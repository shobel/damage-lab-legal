Privacy Policy

Damage Lab · Last updated October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information the Damage Lab mobile game ("the game", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We collect only what the game needs to run, keep your progress safe, and improve the game.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account. When you first play, the game creates an account with a random ID. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number to play.
  • A device ID. A random ID created by the game for this installation, used to keep cloud saves in order.
  • Your game progress. Your save, stage, power, collections and settings, so your progress can be restored on a new device and so leaderboards, guilds and the Arena work.
  • Purchases. When you buy something, the App Store or Google Play processes the payment. We receive a transaction record (what was bought, when, and its price) to deliver the item and prevent duplicate or fraudulent purchases. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • Gameplay events. Events such as sessions, stages cleared, features used and items earned, used to balance and improve the game. They are tied to your anonymous account ID, not to your identity.
  • Chat messages and player names. Messages you send in World, Guild or private chat, and the player and guild names you choose, are stored so other players can see them. Reported messages are reviewed for moderation.
  • Diagnostics. Basic technical information such as the game's version and device platform, used to find and fix problems.
  • Advertising. If you choose to watch an optional video ad for a reward, the ad is provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and similar data to show and measure ads, subject to the consent you give in the game's consent prompts and your device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

How we use it

  • To run the game and its online features (cloud saves, leaderboards, guilds, the Arena, chat and mail).
  • To deliver purchases and restore them if needed.
  • To keep the game fair and safe: detecting cheating, abuse and fraud, and moderating chat.
  • To understand how the game is played and improve it.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites, except as described under Advertising when you consent to it.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the game's servers and database.
  • Apple and Google process purchases made through their stores.
  • Google AdMob provides optional rewarded video ads.

Your choices

  • Delete your account. In the game, open Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, progress, chat messages and other data on our servers. Purchase records are kept without any link to you, as required for refunds and accounting.
  • Ads and tracking. You can decline personalized ads in the game's consent prompt, and control tracking in your device's privacy settings (on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Chat. You can block or report other players from their messages.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us and we will help.

Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. Chat messages are kept for a limited number of recent lines per channel. When you delete your account, your data is deleted from our servers, except anonymized purchase records.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
